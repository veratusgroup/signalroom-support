Signal Room

Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Signal Room is designed to work without a game account or a Veratus-operated game server. This page explains what stays on your iPhone and what happens when you contact us or use Apple's purchase services.

Who is responsible?

Veratus Games is a brand of Veratus Group Limited. For questions about this policy or information you send us, contact sayhello@veratusgames.com.

Information stored by the app

Signal Room stores puzzle progress, decoded evidence, keys, challenge results and your sound and visual settings on your device so play can resume. This data is stored locally using iOS app storage. We do not operate a game server that receives it. Deleting the app may delete this local data; iOS backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Purchases and sharing

The optional Season 1 Pass uses Apple's StoreKit. Apple handles payment and provides purchase-entitlement information to the app so it can unlock content. We do not receive your payment card details through Signal Room. Apple may provide us sales and financial reports in App Store Connect. If you choose to share a game result, iOS presents a share sheet and you decide where it goes.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

The current app does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, a Veratus-operated analytics service, or cross-app tracking. It does not require a game account.

When you contact us

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and any screenshot or information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue and keep a record of the conversation where needed. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or unrelated personal information. You can ask us about or request deletion of your support correspondence at the address above, subject to any records we must retain for legal or operational reasons.

This website

These support pages do not include analytics scripts or advertising. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard connection information to deliver the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change and show the updated date. For privacy questions or requests, email sayhello@veratusgames.com. We cannot remotely inspect or erase progress stored only on your device; Apple handles requests about its own services.
